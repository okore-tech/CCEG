- [P1] Leadership cards are CV dumps: 7/7/4 bullets at 12.5px; 1,727px on mobile before the closing CTA; trust signals buried. Fix: positioning sentence + 2–3 credential chips; full bios behind details or /leadership page. (/impeccable distill)
- [P2] Four-step model duplicated (FlowBand steps vs Pillars, 38px apart, conflicting copy). Fix: merge into Pillars, delete flow-steps. (/impeccable distill)
